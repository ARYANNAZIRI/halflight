No account or sign-in is needed. Everything runs on device.

How to test:
1. Allow camera access. Capture opens immediately.
2. Facing (outer display) needs iPhone Duo with the phone open and the rear camera active. On other devices the Facing tab explains this and offers "Play Motion Cue here".
3. Roll shows the shots taken in this session. Edit opens a photo in the editor.

Photos permission is add-only and requested only when the user saves a shot.
