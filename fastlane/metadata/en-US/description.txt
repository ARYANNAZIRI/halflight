Halflight is a camera for people who photograph people.

Open the phone. The person in front of the camera sees a preview, a motion cue, or a script on the outer screen. You see the viewfinder and the shutter on the inner screen. One session takes you from capture to review to export without leaving the flow.

FACING — THE OUTER SCREEN IS THEIRS
• Mirror: they see themselves before you shoot, with an optional countdown.
• Motion Cue: six original, high-contrast animations that pull a child’s eyes to the camera. Tap to switch, or let Mix shuffle them.
• Prompt: a large scrolling script for talking-head videos. Speed, size, and mirror text from the inner screen.
• Count: a big countdown, an amber wash, then the shot they just took.

A FAST CAMERA
• Photo, video, burst, 3s and 10s timers.
• Main and Ultra Wide, 1x and 2x, pinch to zoom.
• Tap to focus, exposure, grid, level, flash, torch.
• HEIF photos, 4K and HDR video where supported.
• Hardware shutter with the Camera Control or volume buttons.

READY
Halflight watches for a steady, frontal face and pulses “Ready” so you know when to press. Optional Auto-Capture takes the shot for you, always with a two-second warning.

ROLL AND QUICK EDIT
• Sessions keep each story together. Filter by photos, videos, or saved.
• Crop, rotate, exposure, contrast, warmth.
• Arrow, circle, and pen markup, finger or Apple Pencil.
• Save to Photos, share, or copy.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless you share or save it yourself.

Works with iPhone Duo. On other iPhones, Halflight is a fast camera with a roll and an editor, and Motion Cue plays full-screen.
