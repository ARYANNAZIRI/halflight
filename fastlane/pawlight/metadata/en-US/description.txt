Getting a pet to look at the camera is the hard part. Pawlight does it for you.

Tap a sound to get their attention. The moment your cat or dog looks at the lens, Pawlight takes the photo by itself. No more blurry backs of heads, no more waving treats over the phone.

CATCH
Pawlight watches the viewfinder for a cat or dog facing the camera, both eyes and nose in view, and takes the shot the instant it happens. Turn Catch on and leave it running, or tap a sound and Pawlight catches the look that follows.

SOUNDS
Squeak, Kiss, Whistle, Chirp, Crinkle and Bell. Short, sharp sounds made to make ears go up. They play even with your ring switch on silent, and they won't stop your music.

LURES ON IPHONE DUO
On iPhone Duo, the outer screen faces your pet. Pawlight plays a moving lure there, right under the lens: Zip Dot, Bouncy Ball, Feather, Mouse, Bubbles and Peek Paw. Every lure is drawn in blue and yellow, the colours cats and dogs see best, and ends its motion at the camera so their eyes do too.

ROLL
Your shots stay in Pawlight until you save them to Photos or share them.

PAWLIGHT PRO
Free: two lures, two sounds, and one photo per catch.
Pro, one purchase: all six lures, all six sounds, and up to five photos per catch so you get the one with the ears up. Family Sharing supported.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Pet detection runs on your phone, and nothing leaves it unless you share it yourself.
