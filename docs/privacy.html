Halflight Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

Halflight is a camera app. It is built so that nothing you capture ever leaves your phone unless you choose to share or save it.

What Halflight collects

Nothing. Halflight has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Photos and videos you take are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. They are added to your Photos library only when you tap Save.
  • Prompt scripts you write or paste are stored on your phone.
  • Settings are stored on your phone.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Permissions

  • Camera: to show the viewfinder and take photos and video.
  • Microphone: to record sound with video. Photos never use it.
  • Photos (add only): to save shots you choose. Halflight never reads your library.

Face detection

Halflight looks for a steady, frontal face in the viewfinder to show “Ready”. This runs entirely on the device using Apple’s Vision framework. No face data is stored or transmitted.

The outer display

On iPhone Duo, the outer display shows only what you choose for the person in front of the camera: a preview, a motion cue, a script, or a countdown. It never shows your settings or your roll.

Children

Halflight does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
