Pawlight Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

Pawlight is a pet camera. It is built so that nothing you capture ever leaves your phone unless you choose to share or save it.

What Pawlight collects

Nothing. Pawlight has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Photos you take are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. They are added to your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos.
  • Settings are stored on your phone.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Permissions

  • Camera: to show the viewfinder, see your pet, and take photos.
  • Photos (add only): to save shots you choose. Pawlight never reads your library.

Pawlight does not use the microphone.

Pet detection

Pawlight looks for a cat or dog facing the camera so it can take the photo at the right moment. This runs entirely on the device using Apple’s Vision framework. Nothing about the images is stored or transmitted.

Purchases

Pawlight Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on the device.

Children

Pawlight does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
