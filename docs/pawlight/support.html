Pawlight Support

Pawlight is a pet camera. Tap a sound, and it takes the photo the moment your pet looks at the lens.

Common questions

Catch never takes the photo

Catch waits for a cat or dog facing the camera, with both eyes and the nose in view, for about a quarter of a second. Get a little closer, make sure there's light on their face, and tap a sound to make them look up. Check that Catch is on (the green button at the top).

I can't hear the sounds

Pawlight plays sounds even with the ring switch on silent. Check the volume slider in Settings and your phone's volume.

There's no Lure button

Lures play on the outer screen, facing your pet, so they need iPhone Duo with the phone open and the rear camera active. On other iPhones, sounds and Catch still work.

My shots aren’t in Photos

Shots land in Pawlight's Roll first. Open a shot and tap Save to Photos. Pawlight only asks for add-only access to Photos when you do this.

Restoring Pawlight Pro

Settings → Restore Purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.
