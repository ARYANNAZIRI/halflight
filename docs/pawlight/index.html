Pawlight

Your pet looks. The shot's already taken.

Tap a sound to get their attention. The moment your cat or dog looks at the lens, Pawlight takes the photo by itself.

  • Catch: spots a cat or dog facing the camera and takes the shot.
  • Sounds: Squeak, Kiss, Whistle, Chirp, Crinkle and Bell, even on silent.
  • Lures: on iPhone Duo, a moving lure plays on the outer screen, facing your pet.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.
