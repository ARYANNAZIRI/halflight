Halflight Support

Halflight is a camera and director studio for iPhone Duo. The other screen is for them.

Common questions

Facing says “Needs iPhone Duo”

Facing puts a preview, cue, or script on the outer display, which only iPhone Duo has. On other iPhones you can still play a Motion Cue full-screen from the Facing tab.

The Facing toggle is greyed out

Open the phone fully and use the rear camera. Facing is not available with the front camera or when the phone is closed.

My shots aren’t in Photos

Shots land in Roll first. Swipe up on a shot, or use Save, to add it to Photos. Halflight only asks for add-only access to Photos when you do this.

Ready never appears

Ready needs one steady, roughly frontal face that fills at least a small part of the frame for about half a second. It’s deliberately cautious.
